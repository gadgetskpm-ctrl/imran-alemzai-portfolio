Process

Clear from first call to handoff.

Projects move through visible checkpoints. You know what is being built, what is needed from you, and what happens next.

  1. Discover

    Clarify the business problem, audience, priorities, and useful outcome.

  2. Scope

    Agree on deliverables, timing, responsibilities, and price before the build.

  3. Structure

    Organize content, flows, and technical requirements into a practical plan.

  4. Build

    Create the solution in reviewable stages with focused feedback points.

  5. Verify

    Test responsiveness, accessibility, links, content, and essential behavior.

  6. Launch

    Deliver carefully with documentation, handoff, and agreed support.

Common questions

Before we begin.

What does a starting price include?

It is a planning baseline for a focused engagement. Your written scope confirms the exact deliverables, revisions, timing, and total price before work starts.

Do I need a complete technical brief?

No. Explain the business problem or outcome in plain language. ALEMZAI will help turn it into a practical scope.

Can services be combined?

Yes. Website, automation, brand, and implementation work can be combined when the pieces support one clear outcome.

Who owns the finished work?

Ownership and usage rights are documented in the project agreement. Any third-party software, fonts, or licensed assets remain subject to their own terms.

Do you provide support after launch?

Handoff support is included as defined in the scope. Ongoing support can be quoted separately when a project needs it.
